Release of Liability and Assumption of Risk Agreement

Danielle Edmundson · Personal training and coaching · Santa Cruz, California

Danielle Edmundson recommends that you check with your physician before starting any exercise program.

1. Acknowledgement and acceptance of risk

I acknowledge that I have chosen to participate in one or more fitness programs, sessions or services provided by Danielle Edmundson. These may include, but are not limited to, one-on-one personal training, small-group training, group classes, online or virtual coaching, workouts I complete on my own from programming she provides, and nutrition and metabolic health guidance (together, the "Training Programs").

I understand that the Training Programs can be strenuous and may push me to the limits of my physical abilities, and that they carry risks. These risks include, but are not limited to: injury to the muscles, bones, joints, ligaments or tendons; injury to the heart, lungs or circulatory system, which can result in serious injury or death; injury caused by my own actions or those of a training partner or other people nearby; injury caused by improper use or failure of equipment; and injury or illness resulting from a medical condition, whether or not I know about it. I am aware that any of these risks could result in serious injury or death.

2. Assumption of risk and my health

I willingly assume full responsibility for all risks I am exposed to through my participation in Training Programs, and I accept full responsibility for any injury or death that may result. I understand that adverse physical changes can occur during exercise, including abnormal blood pressure, fainting, irregular heart rhythm, stroke, rhabdomyolysis (severe muscle damage) and, in rare cases, heart attack or death.

I have told Danielle Edmundson about any injury, medical condition, medication or pregnancy that could affect my ability to exercise safely, and I will tell her promptly if my health changes. I understand that sessions may take place in private homes (including Danielle Edmundson's home gym), in gyms, or in outdoor and public spaces, and that I may encounter hazards there, including uneven surfaces, traffic, weather, other people and pets.

3. Online coaching and nutrition guidance

I understand that when I follow programming or guidance remotely, including online sessions and workouts I complete on my own, Danielle Edmundson is not present to supervise me, and I am responsible for my own exercise space, equipment and safety.

I understand that nutrition and metabolic health guidance from Danielle Edmundson is educational and is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. I will consult my physician before making significant changes to my diet, especially if I have a medical condition or take medication.

4. Release of liability

In consideration of being allowed to participate in Training Programs, and with full understanding of the risks described above, I voluntarily waive, release, discharge and hold harmless Danielle Edmundson and anyone acting on her behalf from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions or rights of action, or damages of any kind related to, arising from, or in any way connected with my participation in Training Programs, including those allegedly caused by the negligent acts or omissions of the people released.

By signing this agreement, I understand that if I (or any minor on whose behalf I am signing) am hurt, die, or my property is damaged, I am giving up my right to make a claim or file a lawsuit against Danielle Edmundson, even if she negligently causes the injury or damage.

This agreement is binding on me and on my heirs, executors, representatives, successors and assigns. If any part of this agreement is held invalid, the rest of the agreement remains in full force and effect.

5. Indemnification

I accept financial responsibility for any injury I cause to myself or to anyone else through my own negligence. I agree to indemnify, defend and hold harmless Danielle Edmundson and anyone acting on her behalf from liability for injury to or death of any person, and damage to property, that results from my negligent or intentional act or omission while participating in Training Programs. If Danielle Edmundson has to pay attorney's fees or legal costs to enforce this agreement, I agree to reimburse those fees and costs.

6. Photos and video

Danielle Edmundson sometimes takes photos or video during sessions to share on her website and social media. Please choose whether she may use photos, video or other likenesses of the participant for these purposes. I can change my choice at any time by telling her in writing.

7. Parents and legal guardians

As the parent or legal guardian of a participant under 18 years of age, I have read this agreement and voluntarily agree that the minor may participate in Training Programs. I sign this agreement on the minor's behalf and on behalf of the minor's parents and legal guardians, and I agree to all of its terms on their behalf. I give permission for Danielle Edmundson to provide any first aid she considers necessary and, in case of serious illness or injury, to call for medical or surgical care for the minor and to arrange transport to a medical facility.

Acknowledgement

I have read this agreement in its entirety and I fully understand it.
