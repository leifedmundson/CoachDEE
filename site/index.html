More Than a Workout. More Than a Coach.

Hi, I'm Danielle. People call me Coach D.

I bring the experience of a high-level athlete, the expertise of a lifelong coach, and the perspective of a real person navigating real life. I know how to challenge you, how to teach you, and how to meet you exactly where you are. And I genuinely love doing it.

Fitness has given me so much more than physical strength. It's given me confidence, purpose, meaningful relationships, and a career I love. I've experienced the thrill of competition, the discipline of high-level training, and the humbling reality that our bodies, priorities, and lives are always changing.

Those experiences have shaped how I coach.

I love the art and science of coaching. Understanding how bodies move, knowing what to teach, how much to challenge, when to push, and when to adapt. I bring decades of technical knowledge, creativity, and genuine care to every session, but what matters most to me is the relationship we build and how you feel about what you're accomplishing.

You might be an experienced athlete, someone returning to exercise after years away, or someone who isn't quite sure where to begin. You might have ambitious goals or simply want to feel stronger, healthier, and more comfortable in your body.

Wherever you are, we'll start there.

We'll work hard, learn a lot, laugh, and discover what makes movement meaningful for you. I'll listen, teach, challenge, and adjust as we go, helping you build strength, confidence, and capabilities that extend far beyond the gym.

My favorite thing to hear after a session?

“When can we do that again?”

That's when I know we're building something that lasts.

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all training. I believe in the person in front of me.
