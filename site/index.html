More Than a Workout. More Than a Coach.

Hi, I'm Danielle. People call me Coach D.

I bring the experience of a high-level athlete, the expertise of a lifelong coach, and the perspective of a real person navigating real life. I know how to challenge you, how to teach you, and how to meet you exactly where you are. And I genuinely love doing it.

From competing as a Division 1 scholarship athlete to coaching collegiate sports, owning a CrossFit gym, and raising 2 daughters, I’ve spent more than 20 years helping people move, learn, and grow. Fitness has given me so much more than physical strength; it's given me purpose.

I have learned that fitness is about so much more than physical performance.

It's about confidence. Connection. Resilience. Feeling good in your body. Discovering your capabilities. And finding something that makes your life better.

I've experienced the thrill of competition, the discipline of high-level training, the demands of motherhood, and the humbling reality that our bodies and lives are always changing.

Life is ever changing and seasons come and go. I bring that perspective, along with decades of technical knowledge, creativity, and genuine care, to every session.

You might be an experienced athlete, someone who hasn't exercised in years, or someone who isn't quite sure where to begin. You might have big goals or simply want to feel better.

Wherever you are, we'll start there.

We'll work hard, learn a lot, laugh, and find what makes movement meaningful for you.

I'll bring the knowledge, the experience, the creativity, and the care. I'll teach you, challenge you, listen to you, and together we will adjust along the way.

And my hope is that you'll leave feeling a little stronger, a little more confident, and a whole lot more excited.

My favorite thing to hear after a session?

“When can we do that again?”

Because that's when I know we're building something that lasts.

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all training. I believe in the person in front of me.
