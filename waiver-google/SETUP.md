# Online waivers: Google setup

The waiver program runs in **Danielle's** Google account, so emails to clients come from her Gmail.

- `Code.gs`: the program she pastes into script.google.com.
- `Setup instructions.pdf`: her step-by-step guide (about 10 minutes, one time).
- The same two files are packaged for her in **Coach DEE Waiver Setup.zip**.

The program shares the "Coach DEE - Signed Waivers" Drive folder with leifedmundson@gmail.com (edit access) so Leif can help manage it. To change that, edit `SHARE_FOLDER_WITH` near the top of Code.gs.

When she sends the web app address (ends in `/exec`), paste it into `WAIVER_ENDPOINT` near the bottom of `site/waiver.html`.
